## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
PocketVault is your private, beautifully designed pocket for everything worth keeping — notes, links, photos, scans and voice notes — organised, searchable and secure, all without an account.

✨ CAPTURE ANYTHING IN A SECOND
• Notes with checklists, headings and bold text
• Links with rich previews — and an offline copy of the article to read later
• Photos and documents (PDF, text, Office files and more)
• Scan paper documents with automatic edge detection — saved as searchable PDFs
• Voice notes with a live, on-device transcript
• Share to PocketVault from any app, or save instantly with Quick Save
• Home-screen widget for one-tap capture

🧠 SMART, NOT NOSY
• Search like you talk: “receipts over 500 last month”, “links this week”, “#travel”
• Text inside photos and scans is recognised on your device
• Smart actions: call a number, email an address or set a reminder straight from a receipt, card or note
• Spending insights from your receipts, plus weekly activity charts
• Rediscover: an old gem from your vault, resurfaced every day

🔐 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works offline — no account, no ads, no tracking
• App lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Secret vault: AES-256 encrypted items, hidden from your library and search
• Password-protected backups and automatic weekly backups to a folder you choose

🎨 A JOY TO USE
• Vibrant design with smooth animations and haptics
• List and Pinterest-style grid views, pins and colour labels
• Swipe to pin, lock or delete
• Light and dark themes

Your data never leaves your phone unless you export or share it.
```
